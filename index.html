4. Local storage, Android backup and device transfer

StormLift stores its app state in private storage on your device. StormLift does not operate an account server or cloud-sync service for your profile, workout history or progress.

Android Auto Backup remains enabled. If backup is enabled in your Android and Google account settings, Android may automatically upload StormLift app files and settings—including workouts, history, progress, profile information and profile photos—to the backup service associated with your Google account. Android may restore that data after reinstalling the app or during device transfer. YMove catalog and translation cache files are excluded from StormLift’s Android backup rules.

The in-app Delete all StormLift data action deletes app-managed data and caches from the current device and notifies Android that the backup data changed. Android controls when a new backup snapshot replaces an older one. Manage or delete device backups through your Android or Google account settings. Files you previously exported or shared remain wherever you saved or sent them and must be deleted there separately.

5. Third-party services and recipients

  • YMove: licensed exercise names, instructions, images and videos. StormLift sends exercise queries, filters, slugs or identifiers and related request data over HTTPS. Saved profile, body measurements and workout history are not intentionally sent to YMove.
  • Google ML Kit Translation: translates supported exercise guidance on the device after downloading language models. Google states that ML Kit processes device information, app information, configured languages, performance metrics and identifiers for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this SDK data is encrypted in transit and not shared onward by ML Kit.
  • Android and Google services: optional Auto Backup, device transfer, notification delivery, ML Kit model management and Google Play distribution.
  • Remote media hosts: loading YMove-provided thumbnails or videos discloses ordinary network request data such as IP address and device/browser headers to the serving host.
  • Your chosen apps and services: email, file, cloud-storage or sharing providers process data only when you direct an export, share or feedback action to them.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this static legal website and may process ordinary server request logs under GitHub’s own privacy terms.

StormLift Labs does not sell personal data and does not share it for advertising. Third-party providers may process data outside the European Economic Area under their applicable transfer mechanisms and privacy terms.

6. Retention and deletion

App data remains on the device until you edit it, delete individual items, use Delete all StormLift data, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior described above. Translation and exercise cache entries remain until refreshed, cleared or deleted. Feedback email is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records or meet legal obligations.

StormLift has no account-deletion process because it has no user accounts. The in-app deletion action removes profile data, body measurements, the profile photo, workouts, history, checkpoints, preferences, settings and cached app data from the current device.
