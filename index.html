FAQs

What is StormLift?

A free fitness app to build, generate, import and track your workouts.

Is StormLift free?

Yes. StormLift is free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

Who is StormLift for?

Anyone who wants a flexible way to organise and track their training.

Can StormLift generate workouts?

Yes. Generate workouts based on your training setup and preferences.

Can I build my own workouts?

Yes. Create workouts from scratch with full control over every detail.

Can I import my current workouts?

Yes. Import workout plans from Excel, CSV or JSON files.

Can I track my workouts?

Yes. Log sets, reps, weights and completed workouts.

Does StormLift save my progress?

Yes. Your workout history is organised so you can track progress over time.

Can I duplicate workouts?

Yes. Clone workouts or training days to reuse your programming faster.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Home & Travel workouts are designed for limited space and equipment.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete all StormLift-managed data directly from the app.

Is StormLift a medical app?

No. StormLift is a fitness app and does not replace professional healthcare advice.
