4. Local storage, platform backup, device transfer and manual backup

StormLift stores app-managed state in private storage on your device. StormLift does not operate an account server or its own cloud-sync service for your profile, workout history or progress.

Android Auto Backup remains enabled. If backup is enabled in your Android and Google account settings, Android may automatically upload StormLift app files and settings—including workouts, history, progress, profile information and profile photos—to the backup service associated with your Google account. Android may restore that data after reinstalling the app or during device transfer. YMove catalog and translation cache files are excluded from StormLift’s Android backup rules.

On iOS, eligible durable StormLift data in normal app storage—including workouts, history, plans, profile and body information, preferences, settings, onboarding and stored consent versions—may participate in Apple device backup, iCloud backup or device transfer according to your Apple and system configuration. Apple controls those backup and transfer facilities; StormLift does not operate the associated cloud storage.

StormLift may also let you create manual backup or export files containing durable app data, including profile and body data, workouts, history, plans, settings, preferences, onboarding and stored legal consent versions. Restored consent is checked against the requirements current at the time of restore; missing or older consent must be accepted again. Once a manual backup, export or shared file is saved outside StormLift-managed storage, it remains where you saved or sent it and must be deleted there separately.

Translation and exercise cache entries remain until refreshed, cleared or deleted. Feedback email is retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records or meet legal obligations.

StormLift has no account-deletion process because it has no user accounts. The app then returns to onboarding.

Delete All Data does not synchronously delete manual backups, exports or shared files outside StormLift-managed storage; historical Android, Google, Apple or iCloud backup copies controlled by platform infrastructure; system permissions; or unrelated system and account data. Manage platform backup copies through the applicable Google, Android, Apple or iOS settings. If you later restore a manual backup, restored consent is checked against the then-current requirements.
