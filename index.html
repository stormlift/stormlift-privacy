StormLift Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

StormLift is provided by StormLift. This Privacy Policy explains how StormLift handles information when you use the app.

StormLift is a fitness planning and workout tracking app. StormLift is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or rehabilitation guidance.

Information Stored on Your Device

StormLift stores app data locally on your device so the app can save your training plans, workouts, progress, profile, settings, and active workout state.

This may include generated workouts, imported or custom workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, rest times, cardio duration, pace, incline, workout completion, workout history, progress data, and resumable workout checkpoints.

Workout and Fitness Data

StormLift uses workout and fitness data to provide core app features such as workout planning, workout tracking, progress tracking, workout history, active session tracking, rest timers, and training reminders.

Based on the current app implementation, StormLift does not upload your saved workout history, profile data, or progress data to a StormLift-operated cloud account.

Profile Data

If you enter profile information, StormLift may store your display name, training goal, gender, age, weight, height, preferred weight unit, theme, date format, reminder settings, and related app preferences on your device.

Profile Photo

If you choose a profile photo, StormLift stores a copy in the app’s private storage on your device. The photo may also be included in user-created backup files.

Imported and Exported Files

StormLift lets you import workout files and export workouts or app backups. These actions are user-initiated.

Exported files may contain workout, profile, progress, and settings data depending on the export type. You control where exported files are saved or shared.

Exercise Content and YMove

StormLift uses YMove exercise content to support exercise search, exercise catalog lookup, exercise details, thumbnails, and videos.

StormLift may send exercise-related requests to YMove, including exercise search terms, muscle groups, exercise slugs, pagination values, and media-related parameters.

Based on the current app implementation, StormLift does not upload your saved workout history, profile data, or progress data to YMove.

Remote Thumbnails and Videos

StormLift may load remote exercise thumbnails and videos from URLs provided by YMove. Loading this media may cause your device to connect to those remote media hosts.

Notifications and Reminders

StormLift uses notifications for active workout/rest timer updates and optional training reminders. Reminder scheduling happens locally on your device.

Notification permission is requested when needed for these features.

Feedback Emails

If you send feedback from the app, StormLift opens your email app.

The feedback email may include your selected feedback category, your message, app version, Android version, device model, and timestamp.

Feedback is sent to:

stormliftapp@gmail.com

Android Backup and Device Transfer

Android backup and device transfer may include StormLift’s local app data, such as workout data, profile data, settings, progress, and profile images.

The YMove exercise catalog cache is excluded from Android backup and device transfer.

Data Deletion

You can delete workouts and reset training data inside the app.

You can also remove local StormLift data by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

If you created exported backup files, you must delete those files from wherever you saved them.

StormLift does not currently use user accounts, so account deletion does not apply.

No Account, Login, or Cloud Sync

StormLift does not currently include account creation, login, or StormLift-operated cloud sync.

No Ads

StormLift does not currently include advertising SDKs.

No Purchases

StormLift does not currently include in-app purchases or subscriptions.

No Analytics or Crash Reporting SDK

StormLift does not currently include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or similar analytics or crash reporting SDKs.

Children and Target Audience

StormLift is not designed as a children-directed app. If you are under the age required by your country or region to use fitness apps independently, use StormLift only with permission and supervision from a parent or guardian.

Fitness and Safety Notice

StormLift provides general fitness planning, tracking, and informational guidance. It does not provide medical advice or guarantee results.

Before beginning or changing an exercise program, consult a qualified healthcare professional if you have medical concerns, injuries, pregnancy, symptoms, or any condition that may affect exercise safety.

Train within your ability, use proper technique, and stop exercising if something feels wrong.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last updated.
