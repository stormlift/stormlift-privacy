# StormLift website asset manifest

- `header/`: compact header mark.
- `hero-slider/`: three reusable grayscale people, store badges, and the large STORMLIFT wordmark artwork.
- `app-phones/`: left Progress, center Adaptive Workouts, and right Profile phone renders.
- `workouts/`: Quick Workout, Workout Journey, Home & Travel, Import Workout, and Custom Workout cut-outs.
- `faqs/`: accessible accordion plus and minus icons.
- `icons/`: 100% Free, No Ads, No Subscriptions, and Your Data benefit icons.
- `final-cta/`: small Progress and large Adaptive Workouts phone renders.
- `footer/`: StormLift lockup, Instagram icon, and X icon.
- `fonts/`: Outfit Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, and ExtraBold, reused from the StormLift brand assets.

The Focus, Track, and Adaptive Coach sections reuse the three hero people. Their gradient panels, glows, pills, and floating blocks are CSS-generated.
