Important information

Training Safety

For StormLift users aged 18 and over

General health notice

StormLift provides general fitness planning and tracking. It is not a medical device; it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease; and it does not replace qualified healthcare advice.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing exercise if you have an injury, symptoms, pregnancy or a health concern.

Exercise risk

Exercise involves inherent risk. Choose appropriate exercises, loads, technique, warm-up, progression, recovery and surroundings.

Stop immediately and seek appropriate professional advice if you experience pain, dizziness, injury or unusual symptoms.

Adaptive workout features

The Adaptive Workout Generator, Adaptive Coach, Workout Journey and related features produce automated, rules-based outputs from the information and preferences you provide. They are not represented as artificial intelligence.

Review each workout, exercise, load and progression before use. Outputs are general fitness information, not individualized medical prescriptions or guarantees of safety, performance or results.

Age requirement

StormLift is intended only for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. Do not use the app if you are under 18.
